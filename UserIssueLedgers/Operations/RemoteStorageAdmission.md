# User Issue Ledger: Operations / remote storage admission

| ID                                          | Applies to                               | Mistake pattern                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Required behavior                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Prevention and verification                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| UIL-OPERATIONS-REMOTE-STORAGE-ADMISSION-001 | Multi-slot remote FAST and RANS refill   | A remote refill first used one pre-loop disk snapshot and later still treated a flat 24 GiB “next job” allowance as sufficient before submitting an 18–22-angle FAST sweep whose exact remaining-work exposure was materially larger. Accepted jobs could therefore drive the filesystem onto a restrictive trajectory while CPU appeared fully utilized. | Every remote FAST or RANS candidate must first become a durable `pending/submitting` reservation so its exact fidelity and case count enter the storage exposure, then pass a fresh disk measurement before the engine call. After every successful submission, remeasure again and stop the refill immediately when denied. Existing jobs continue and reconciliation/reclaim stay live; never weaken the reserve or emergency ceiling merely to fill configured CPU slots. | Regressions require the pre-submit guard to observe the exact reserved candidate and deny it without an engine call or upstream-lease cancellation, then give both lanes multiple independently runnable jobs, deny a post-submit refresh, and require exactly the accepted prefix with no later claim. Production burn-in pairs real OpenFOAM/CPU saturation with a non-decreasing safety margin relative to computed remaining work. |
| UIL-OPERATIONS-REMOTE-STORAGE-ADMISSION-002 | Detached terminal remote generations     | The transfer loop deferred already-detached terminal directory deletion until after more expensive legacy reconciliation and deleted only five per pass. A large zero-owner backlog could therefore grow faster than cleanup while live CFD consumed the final filesystem reserve.                                                                | Before any potentially slow transfer or legacy reconciliation, delete a bounded high-throughput batch of terminal remote engine directories whose database job is stable, cancellation is not in flight, and both result and attempt ownership are already absent. The engine execution lock remains the final deletion guard; accepted, attached, live, and cancellation-pending generations never enter this path.                                     | Regression gives the cleaner more disposable rows than its requested limit plus attached/live/cancellation-pending guards, requires deletion of exactly the bounded zero-owner prefix, and verifies the other rows remain. Production verification must show the zero-owner backlog and disk-use slope falling while all live OpenFOAM children remain unchanged.                          |
| UIL-OPERATIONS-REMOTE-STORAGE-ADMISSION-003 | Reconciliation-discovered retry routes   | Reconciliation could both persist a FAST recovery route and submit its physical child before the scheduler reached the admission fence, disk reserve, and aggregate-capacity gates. A terminal RANS result could therefore start a large FAST retry even while storage admission was already closed.                                              | Ordinary scheduler reconciliation is always route-only: it may ingest evidence and durably record exact retry, promotion, continuation, or clean-restart ownership, but it cannot submit replacement CFD. Only the subsequent admission lanes may create a physical child after the current admission fence, disk measurement, engine capability, and aggregate-capacity checks all pass.                                                                | A scheduler-option regression requires `recordRoutesOnly=true` even when a caller omits or explicitly clears it, while preserving job scopes and failure-recovery controls. Production verification must show a disk-blocked tick can ingest and route terminal evidence without another engine submission.                                                                                |
| UIL-OPERATIONS-REMOTE-STORAGE-ADMISSION-004 | Active-promise failed source generations | Reclaim required the whole remote promise to be terminal even when an exact failed generation was explicitly nonpublishable and every attached attempt had already handed ownership to a durable pending/running FAST obligation. Hundreds of obsolete source directories therefore remained while their fresh recovery owners consumed new disk. | An active promise may retain its scheduling lease while an obsolete failed source generation is deleted only when every attached attempt is explicitly nonpublishable, every attempt is the exact source of a pending/running PRECALC obligation, no result lacks an owned attempt, and no accepted, canonical, delivery, verification, or live-continuation owner exists. The obligation and source incident survive with their source pointer cleared. | Regression proves an active promise with exact pending FAST ownership reclaims the old source once, retains the obligation/action/incident history, and permits source-less fresh recovery. Guards preserve mixed/unclassified attempts, ownerless result projections, accepted/canonical evidence, cells without an exact active obligation, and any live continuation or delivery.       |
| UIL-OPERATIONS-REMOTE-STORAGE-ADMISSION-005 | Closed-promise retained evidence           | Generic retention required a reclaimed hub binding receipt for every remote result and attempt. A terminal lease with publication-owned evidence or without an authoritative disposal acknowledgement could therefore retain tens of GiB of reproducible OpenFOAM working state per job and force the disk gate to strand otherwise usable CPU capacity. | When deletion authority is absent but the exact promise is terminal and no delivery or signed reclaim is live, compact—never delete—the terminal engine directory through the authenticated full-strip boundary. The engine must stream and authenticate every archive relied on by every evidence directory; accepted attempts, classifications, result projections, manifests, archives, stored media, and incident history remain intact. Active promises and live continuation/transfer owners remain protected, and any unknown retained entry leaves `stripped_at` null and rotates behind untouched candidates. | DB-backed regressions require a closed-promise retained generation to request a full strip while preserving its result and attempt, require active/in-flight remote work to remain untouched, and require a partial engine report with unknown entries to remain retryable without monopolizing the batch. Production verification compares exact protected ownership before/after, engine strip reports, physical free bytes, and unchanged live OpenFOAM children. |
| UIL-OPERATIONS-REMOTE-STORAGE-ADMISSION-006 | Authoritatively cancelled unpublished generations | A local `accepted` classifier label was treated as permanent retention authority even when the upstream promise cancellation had been durably acknowledged and the generation had no canonical selection, hub binding, or fulfilled promise-point owner. The retained archive itself—not removable working state—was therefore large enough to keep the remote disk permanently fenced. | Apply the recorded disposable-generation policy to the complete exact job only after every attempt has an explicit verdict, the upstream promise is fulfilled or its cancellation outbox is delivered, and no attempt owns a canonical selection, hub receipt, fulfilled point, live delivery, continuation, verification, or PRECALC recovery. Local acceptance alone is not publication. Retain result shells and incidents, but remove unpublished attempts/artifacts/media and the locked terminal engine directory. | A must-catch regression proves an acknowledged cancellation reclaims one locally accepted but unpublished generation and clears its coefficient projection; the paired false-positive guard proves a local terminal flag without authoritative acknowledgement is retained. Canonical-selection, hub-binding, fulfilled-point, active-promise, in-flight transfer, continuation, verification, PRECALC, and unclassified-attempt guards remain mandatory. Production dry-run counts the exact set before deploy and compares deleted job IDs/bytes with the unchanged protected-owner counts. |
