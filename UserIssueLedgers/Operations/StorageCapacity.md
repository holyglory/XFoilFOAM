| UIL-OPERATIONS-STORAGE-CAPACITY-001 | Hub and remote-solver CFD storage          | Reproducible solver working data accumulated without a hard bounded-lifecycle guarantee until the remote Docker filesystem reached 100%, PostgreSQL became unhealthy, the remote heartbeat stopped, and campaign scheduling blocked. Terminal rejected-only jobs were excluded from delivery settlement, and promoted wave-1 parents were permanently excluded even after their wave-2 replacement settled, so neither received the job-level acknowledgement required for stripping. | CFD working data is disposable and automatically reclaimed. A solver must stop admitting new work before its writable filesystem reserve is threatened, aggressively remove cancelled, failed, superseded, acknowledged, and otherwise restartable working generations, and resume only after measured headroom is restored. Valid generations are delivered first; terminal rejected points are released for a fresh promise and their job receives a reclamation acknowledgement. A promoted parent remains protected while any exact replacement obligation or child is active, then receives a superseded acknowledgement after every obligation and child is terminal. Canonical non-solver configuration and reconnect credentials remain preserved. | Regression-test admission and cleanup with active, accepted, mixed accepted/rejected, rejected-only, terminal, acknowledged, cancelled, malformed, orphaned, and promoted parent/child jobs; include a slow or failed delivery path. Pin the persisted `conditionalPromotionId` child-provenance field; prove an active child or retryable obligation blocks parent reclamation, while terminal obligations plus acknowledged children unblock it without publishing superseded RANS. In production, verify actual filesystem headroom, healthy PostgreSQL/Redis, each host's configured slot limit, fresh heartbeats, renewed promises, real solve progress, valid-generation delivery, rejected-point release, parent/child job-level acknowledgement, and post-terminal disk reclamation. |
